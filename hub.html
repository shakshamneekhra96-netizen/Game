For my fake friend

I know you don't care much about these things, but some people genuinely worry about you. That's why they ask you what changed. They're not asking because they have nothing better to do or because they want to annoy you. If you genuinely care about someone, you would probably do the same, maybe even more than I do. And when someone genuinely asks you about something, it doesn't mean they expect you to tell them everything or explain every little thing.

And I know sometimes when someone asks you something, your first thought might be, "Why do you want to know? What are you going to do with that? Why do you need to know so much?" Or maybe even, "Sab bataun teko kya?" I know, sabko aap sab nahi bata sakti… I understand that. But for me, it's… leave it. There can be a lot of thoughts like that, and honestly, I think we're quite similar in that way. Even when my mom asks me something, mere mind mein bhi yehi thoughts aate hain.

But remember one thing: try to see the intention behind the question. Trust me, you won't feel as irritated when you understand that the person is asking because they genuinely care, not because they want to interfere, control you, or annoy you.

But when you suddenly change or become distant, people may worry. It's not about wanting to know everything you're doing. Sometimes, just telling a friend a little bit is enough to keep them from worrying. It doesn't matter if the reason sounds silly, and you don't have to make up an excuse either. Whether you were thinking about the mountains, needed a little break, or simply didn't feel like talking, saying something is better than leaving people wondering.

I'll tell you one secret. When you're thinking about something that's not part of your mountain plans or anything you've mentioned, sometimes it shows. Your hand gestures are different, your eyes say a lot, and even your tone has a slightly different feel to it. Your whole way of being changes a little.

But anyway, I believe in you. Maybe you're lying, maybe you're making an excuse, or maybe you genuinely have a reason. It's always in your hands to choose whether you tell the truth or lie. In the end, it's your choice whether you want to break someone's trust or protect it.

And honestly, I only trust what you tell me. Whatever is running through my mind, whatever I might be thinking, I still choose to believe what you say. So whether it's the truth, a small excuse, or something you simply don't want to share, it's up to you. I don't trust just anyone like that, especially not to this extent.

You don't have to explain everything. Sometimes, even a simple "I need some time" or "I'm not feeling great right now" is enough. At least then, the other person knows something is going on and doesn't have to assume the worst.

Anyway, try to trust people and remember the intention behind what they say. Sometimes people may say things in the wrong way, but that doesn't always mean they have a bad intention.

And I know I'm not always right either. Neither are you. We're both going to misunderstand things sometimes. Just remember that when someone genuinely cares about you, their concern usually comes from that care, not from wanting to control you or know everything about you.

So please understand what I'm trying to say. Use your 2gm little brain for once. 😂
